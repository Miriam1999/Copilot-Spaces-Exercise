# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work with **QA/Testing Lead** on test strategy and acceptance criteria validation
- Collaborate with **Engineering Lead/Tech Lead** on architecture decisions and technical guidance
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on task status and schedule updates

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Report to and receive guidance from **Product Lead** on strategy alignment
- Work with **Sponsors** for prioritization decisions and resource negotiations
- Engage **Stakeholders** for requirements input and validation
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on scheduling and dependencies

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Escalate blockers to **Sponsors** for removal of organizational barriers
- Communicate with **Product Lead** on scope and timeline decisions
- Coordinate with **Developers** and **Engineering Lead/Tech Lead** on capacity and technical dependencies
- Engage **QA/Testing Lead** on quality timelines and test coverage status
- Work with **Stakeholders** to maintain alignment on project progress

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation. They collaborate with product and delivery teams to ensure quality standards are met and features work as intended.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategy and test approach for the project
- Create and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Validate that features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Report quality metrics and test coverage to the team

### Goals
- Ensure high product quality and user satisfaction
- Catch defects early in the development cycle
- Maintain comprehensive test coverage
- Reduce post-release issues and customer impact

### Typical Communication
- Participation in planning and kickoff meetings
- Sprint standups and QA sync meetings
- Test plans and quality reports
- Defect triage and issue escalation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Developers** to understand design and implementation for effective test coverage
- Work with **Product Managers** to define acceptance criteria and success metrics
- Coordinate with **Project Managers** on testing timelines and blockers
- Report quality status and risks to **Engineering Lead/Tech Lead** and project stakeholders
- Support **Sponsors** with quality metrics for go/no-go release decisions

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide senior product oversight, strategic direction, and guidance to Product Managers and delivery teams. They ensure alignment with broader product vision and organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Provide product strategy and vision guidance
- Mentor and support Product Managers
- Review and approve product charters and roadmaps
- Escalate critical prioritization decisions
- Ensure cross-functional alignment on product initiatives
- Approve go/no-go decisions at key project gates

### Goals
- Align product work with organizational strategy
- Develop and mentor product management capability
- Ensure customer value and competitive advantage
- Enable consistent decision-making across initiatives

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and delivery teams
- Project charter reviews and gate approvals
- Stakeholder and executive briefings
- Escalation and risk discussions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Guide and mentor **Product Managers** on strategy and prioritization
- Collaborate with **Sponsors** on business alignment and resource needs
- Engage with **Engineering Lead/Tech Lead** on technical feasibility and innovation opportunities
- Participate in gate reviews with **Project Managers** and cross-functional teams
- Provide strategic input to **Stakeholders** on product direction

---

## Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors are executive or business leaders who provide business alignment, resources, and high-level priority decisions. They remove blockers and ensure organizational support for project success.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business justification and priority alignment
- Allocate budget and resources for the project
- Approve major scope, timeline, and resource changes
- Remove organizational blockers and dependencies
- Escalate critical business or strategic issues
- Participate in project reviews at key milestones

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain strategic alignment with organizational goals
- Remove barriers to project success
- Provide accountability for business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Project charter review and approval
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Critical issue escalations
- Milestone reviews and gate decisions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Approve project charters reviewed by **Product Lead** and **Project Managers**
- Make prioritization decisions based on input from **Product Managers** and **Stakeholders**
- Approve scope and resource changes requested by **Project Managers**
- Review quality and readiness for release with **QA/Testing Lead**
- Authorize escalations from **Project Managers** to remove organizational obstacles

---

## Engineering Lead / Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads provide technical oversight, architecture guidance, and risk assessment. They ensure technical excellence and guide engineering teams on complex design decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical architecture and design decisions
- Assess technical risks and propose mitigations
- Guide developers on complex technical challenges
- Ensure code quality and technical standards compliance
- Coordinate with other engineering teams on dependencies
- Contribute to capacity planning and resource estimation

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and risk
- Build engineering capability and knowledge sharing
- Ensure alignment with organizational tech strategy

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Sprint planning and estimation sessions
- Code reviews and technical guidance
- Cross-team technical coordination

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Mentor and guide **Developers** on technical decisions and best practices
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** and **Product Lead** on technical feasibility of features
- Provide **Project Managers** with technical risk assessments and capacity estimates
- Work with **QA/Testing Lead** on testability and quality strategy
- Communicate technical readiness and risks to **Sponsors** at gate reviews

---

## Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Stakeholders are business and cross-functional representatives who provide input, approvals, and direction on project decisions. Stakeholder groups vary by project but typically include representatives from affected business areas.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business and operational context for requirements
- Review and approve requirements and acceptance criteria
- Participate in project reviews and milestone gates
- Provide feedback on feature usability and business fit
- Escalate business risks and dependencies
- Advocate for their function in prioritization decisions

### Goals
- Ensure project meets business needs and functional requirements
- Minimize rework through early feedback and alignment
- Support smooth adoption and deployment
- Maintain organizational alignment on project direction

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and stakeholder meetings
- Requirements reviews and feedback sessions
- Monthly status updates and milestone reviews
- Issue escalations and decision requests

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Provide requirements input to **Product Managers** during planning and prioritization
- Receive status updates from **Project Managers** and escalation from **Sponsors**
- Give feedback on acceptance criteria and feature fitness to **QA/Testing Lead**
- Participate in gate reviews coordinated by **Project Managers** and approved by **Sponsors**
- Advocate for dependencies and risks to **Product Lead** and **Sponsors**
